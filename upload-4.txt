Please create a game where you run a 2D pizza shop using just a single HTML file. Players take on the role of the owner (view from inside the shop) without actually cooking the pizza. The initial price per pizza at your shop is around 1350 yen, though this varies significantly based on the quantity and types of toppings the customer orders. The price also fluctuates greatly due to factors like cooking equipment upgrades affecting quality and staff skill levels. You can hire staff, upgrade pizza-making tools, ovens, stoves, etc., serve customers, and even offer a full delivery service (display delivery fees, labor costs, and tips upfront, deducting them from profits). When pizza toppings (13 types available) run out, you must restock them (each topping has a maximum inventory of 300). You start with 45,000 yen and just one staff member at the counter. From there, you can increase staff to a maximum of 30, expand the shop size, and place more furniture inside. (Placing furniture inside the store attracts more customers. The logic for attracting customers based on furniture is that they gather if the combined grade and style score of the store's furniture is high. Furniture has 5 grades; each higher grade corresponds to increasingly expensive furniture. Style relates to cost performance.). Staff should display individual names, specialties (showing the permanent efficiency boost percentage for each specialty), and stamina at the staff bar. They can be upgraded up to level 20. In-game cooking equipment can be upgraded (usually up to level 50 for all items). Each upgrade improves efficiency and gradually increases actual sales. Advertising is also available (with daily plans ranging from ¥1,000 and ¥2,000 to ¥4,000, ¥5,000, ¥8,500, ¥12,000, ¥23,000, ¥34,000, ¥43,000, ¥60,000, and ¥75,000. The higher the amount, the more customers interested in the higher tiers will be attracted). Smooth, high-resolution animations. Set the game's day to last 40 minutes in real time, with a maximum of 5,000 customers per day and a maximum of 20,000 deliveries per day. Implement pseudo-internet features: display the “BASE G” app where customers rate the shop's reputation on a 10-star scale, and the “KX 2” app where experts visiting roughly every 5 days provide professional evaluations. KX 2 is a paid service costing ¥15,000 per month. Subscribing grants access to expert evaluations that assess not only taste and texture but also the shop's interior, atmosphere, staff attitude, and business operations on a 20-point scale. (Shops cannot be evaluated if experts have not visited yet or have only visited a few times.) Since this evaluation is visible to customers unfamiliar with the shop, it significantly influences whether customer numbers decrease or new/repeat customers increase. BASE G displays customer reviews and ratings, updated in real time. (Similar to KX 2, during the early stages when customers are inconsistent or simply don't come often, the number of reviews and ratings will naturally be zero or very low.) The shop interior is naturally finished to a high quality. There are 4 types of furniture, with 8 pieces each. Staff members have distinct personalities, with detailed settings like whether they have a tendency to slack off based on their stamina, or if they are hardworking or not. If funds are insufficient, a feature allowing borrowing up to 2 million yen with interest is implemented. A mobile phone is implemented, with a UI that closely resembles an actual mobile phone. The mobile phone function includes “money R,” a site for financial advice, and “MESSAGE,” an app for sending messages to friends and family. Here, you can converse with various characters and borrow money from different people within these conversations. If you don't repay borrowed money, the interest will accumulate rapidly. Provide a separate settings button for fine-tuning game parameters. Pressing this button should enable: “One-time 50,000 yen loan button,” “Staff ability limit break mode,” and “Game graphics settings.” Add smooth, lifelike motion and animation to characters and staff actions. Detailed depictions of kitchen areas like stoves and countertops should include realistic staff working motions. Cooking efficiency, speed, and quality should be upgradeable through cooking equipment enhancements. Furniture and other design graphics should also be rendered with professional-grade detail.
